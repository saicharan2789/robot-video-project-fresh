Welcome to the Sheffield Mental Health Guide

A website for Sheffield people of all ages

We’re here to provide information on mental health services, activities and resources that are available to you, or someone you are supporting. Our website has been co-created with the community and people who have lived experience of mental health.

Service categories

  • Abuse & trauma

    This category provides information about services for individuals who have experienced abuse or trauma. Such as emotional, physical, or sexual abuse or traumatic events like accidents or disasters.

  • Alcohol, drugs & addiction

    This category provides information about services for individuals experiencing alcohol, drug, or other substance-related issues.

  • Autism, ADHD & learning disabilities

    This category provides information about services for individuals with autism spectrum disorder (ASD), attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder (ADHD), and other neurodivergent conditions.

  • Bereavement

    This category provides information about services for individuals experiencing grief, including specialised grief support for those who are bereaved by suicide.

  • Culturally specific

    This category provides information about services for individuals from specific cultural backgrounds and provides information about services that provide culturally sensitive support.

  • Eating disorders

    This category provides information about services for individuals struggling with eating disorders, such as anorexia, bulimia, or binge eating disorder.

  • Employment, debt & benefits

    This category provides information about services for individuals facing employment challenges, financial difficulties, and issues related to benefits, welfare support and the cost of living crisis.

  • Families & carers

    This category provides information about services for families and caregivers of people struggling with their mental health.

  • Housing issues

    This category provides information about services for people who are experiencing housing-related challenges, including homelessness, eviction, or housing instability.

  • LGBTQIA+

    This category provides information about services specifically designed for the LGBTQIA+ community.

  • Older people

    This category provides information about services for older people, addressing their health, social, and emotional well-being.

  • Physical health & disability

    This category provides information about services for individuals dealing with various physical health challenges and disabilities. Additionally, it includes resources related to sexual health.

  • Pregnancy & parenting

    This category provides information about services supporting individuals during pregnancy or parenting that focus on supporting individuals wellbeing and mental health.

  • Problems with a service

    This category provides information about who to contact about complaints or problems with health care services.

  • Refugees & asylum seekers

    This category provides information about services that support individuals who have been displaced from their home countries and are seeking refuge or asylum.

  • Relationship support

    This category provides information about services for individuals, couples or families experiencing challenges or seeking guidance in their relationships.

  • Specific mental health condition

    This category provides information about services for individuals dealing with a particular mental health condition, such as depression, anxiety, bipolar disorder, etc.

  • Veterans

    This category provides information about services for military veterans and their families.
